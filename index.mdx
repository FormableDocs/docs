The Formable API lets you collect signatures without building the signing experience from scratch. Upload a document once as a **template**, send it as a **signature request**, and get it signed: either inside your product, or by email.

<Tip>
  You can try the API without writing code. Open the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction), add your bearer token on any endpoint page, and send real requests from the docs playground.
</Tip>

## What you can build

- **Embedded e-signature** — Host signing inside your product via iframe. See [Embedded signing](/walkthroughs/embedded-signing).
- **Templates** — Upload a document and place fields in the editor for your signers to fill.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Upload a PDF, DOC, or DOCX with [Create a template](/api-reference/endpoint/create-template). Formable returns a `templateId` you reuse for every send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a signature request">
    Start an [embedded signature request](/api-reference/endpoint/create-signature-request) from the template with `signers`, then generate a [signing URL](/api-reference/endpoint/create-signing-url) for each recipient to embed in your product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retrieve the signed PDF">
    When signing finishes, wait for the [`document_completed`](/webhooks/events#document_completed) webhook, then fetch the [signed envelope](/api-reference/endpoint/get-signed-envelope).
  </Step>
</Steps>
